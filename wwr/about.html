Raging Waters Rafting Co.

At Raging Waters, our mission is to deliver unforgettable, high-adrenaline river adventures while upholding the highest standards of safety, environmental stewardship, and customer care. We empower adventurers of all skill levels to discover the wild.

History

Founded in 1998 by a group of passionate river guides, Raging Waters Rafting Co. began with a single raft and a dream to share the excitement of whitewaters. Over the decades, we have grown into a premier adventure outfit, guiding thousands of happy rafters down the world's most thrilling rivers while supporting local conservation efforts.
